Social & proposals

Two panels and one Diamond. Social is where you tell us something. Improve is the board it arrives on. Pending is where anything waiting on you turns up.

Saying something

Open Social and write. The first line is the title, and what happened goes under it. Send sends what is on screen and the build id, and nothing else goes with it.

Posting needs a posting name, which the app calls your voice. Without one you can read everything and keep a note to this device. The panel says so in one row, Set a posting name to send., with Get one beside it. It takes one tap, and it is part of Daimond Pro.

A note that can be acted on

A note needs three things, in this order. Leave any of them out and the note needs a conversation before anything can be done.

  • Where it is. Which panel, and which part of it.
  • What you expected.
  • What happened instead.

The build id is attached for you, so you need not find it.

Naming where

There are four regions, and they are the ones this guide uses throughout: the top bar, the rail on the left, the centre, and the dock on the right. On a phone, the drawer is the rail and a sheet is a dock panel.

Inside a region, these are the words worth knowing.

  • chip, the small pill in the top bar.
  • tile, a Diamond, a chat or an agent in a list.
  • head, a section's heading row.
  • cog, the settings for the thing it sits on.
  • light, the round one beside a play and a pause.
  • composer, the message box and its buttons.
  • row and dialog, which mean what they say.

"The cog on the Life log tile" is unambiguous, and "the settings button" is not.

The board

Improve shows what has been proposed and what is being done about it, in four columns: Awaiting you, Greenlit (accepted, not built yet), Shipped, and Dropped. Reading needs nothing. Voting and commenting need a posting name.

Only we can add or move a card, and the board says nothing about who decides. If it cannot be reached it says Could not load. and nothing more, because that is all it knows.

Pending

Pending, in the dock, holds what is waiting on you, which is a daimon asking permission for something, and proposals about your own account. Sort by priority, newest or oldest. Each row is answered one of three ways, Do it, Discuss it first, or Drop it. Empty, it says Nothing waiting on you.

The Optimiser

Your rail holds a Diamond called Daimond Optimiser. It finds what should have been written down, what should have been cheaper and what should have been automatic, and hands you the change to make.

It reads counts kept on this device, meaning turns, spend, models and tool calls, and nothing else. It does not see what any conversation was about.

It ships stopped, and only you can start it. Open its settings with the cog on its tile, find the action After 30 minutes of your activity, and press play on the light beside it. After that it reads the counts every half hour you actually work.

What it finds arrives as a tile in Pending, wherever you happen to be, with a headline, the evidence under it, and Do it. The note on the tile says where it came from, Found by its Diamond. “Do it” asks that Diamond to carry it out. Nothing is held open by one, so the tick is a fresh instruction, not a permission you gave earlier.

The Optimiser alone is denied two things, because it runs while nobody is looking. It cannot publish in your name, and it cannot dispatch helpers.

People, messages and sharing

People connects you to somebody directly. Show your code to them in the room, or read theirs. From there you have private messages between the two of you, and groups. A group can be closed by whoever made it, and then nothing already said is taken away and nothing new arrives.

A Diamond can be shared with somebody you are connected to. The shared Diamond carries its memory and its page, and both are sealed to the people you sent it to.

Each of these depends on the relay being switched on. Where it is not, the panel says so in one line, and offers no control that would fail.
