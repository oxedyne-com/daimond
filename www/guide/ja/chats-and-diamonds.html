チャットと diamond

A chat is a conversation. A diamond is a saved project with its own memory, its own page and its own files. You keep the one inside the other.

A chat

+ — 隣は チャット starts one. Type in the box and send. A new chat is titled 新しいチャット until you say something, and after that its first line is its title.

While an answer is arriving you can keep typing. A message sent then waits as a dashed bubble and goes as its own turn when the answer finishes. Click it to take it back, or its cross to drop it. Pressing stop hands anything queued back to you, unsent.

Deleting a chat moves it to the Trash, so nothing asks first. The two acts that cannot be undone are the ones that ask, and both are in that panel.

Reading a long thread

Every turn is a tile, labelled You, Thinking, Tool, Daimond, or as a note from the app itself. Each header carries the local date and time to the minute in the Holocene reckoning, so 12026-09-15 13:12 is this year plus ten thousand. Hover for the full instant with its offset.

The header of the chat carries the controls.

  • Show steps shows the work done between your message and the answer.
  • Collapse answers leaves only what you asked. It is the quickest way to skim, and how you start picking turns.
  • Short answers is a standing request for brevity in this chat. It is a file in your workspace that you can edit, not a hidden instruction.
  • The permission chip reads Guarded, Ask every time or Bypass. See 安全性と対処.

Two chevrons beside the send button walk back through your own messages and return to the end.

What a stop looks like

A turn says how it ended, in exact words.

  • Answered means it finished and said something.
  • Stopped means you pressed stop.
  • Step limit reached means it ran out of rounds.
  • Ended without saying anything means the model returned nothing at all.
  • Ended without an answer, after reasoning means it thought, was asked once to answer or use a tool, and still said nothing.
  • Ended on an error、または Ended: the tool call arrived as text, say what they say.

None of these is dressed up as an answer.

Keeping a chat

Two buttons sit on a chat's row in the rail. Keep turns the whole conversation into a diamond. Fold all turns it into a diamond by summarising it, keeping what was worked out and dropping the back-and-forth.

To keep only part, collapse the answers, tick the turns worth keeping, and choose 選択分をたたむ. Fold a step you have adopted, and leave a passing remark where it is.

Nothing is absorbed on its own. A diamond's memory changes only through a fold or an edit, and every write is kept in History.

Inside a diamond

Your diamonds sit above your chats, and the + makes one. A diamond has two faces, and a switch in its header moves between them. 結晶 is what it knows, and チャット is its own conversation.

The card lists the diamond's title and summary in its own words, then everything else it holds, a line each. Edit opens a form. Show raw memory opens the file itself, for when you would rather work in it directly.

The four files a diamond keeps

Memory is kept in the open. Four files sit in the diamond's own folder, readable in the Workspace panel and by the daimon.

  • crystal.json holds what this diamond is, meaning its title, summary, sections, facts and links.
  • REQUIREMENTS.md holds what is to do, as objectives with ranked tasks under each. A task leaves it by being ticked or struck out, and by nothing else.
  • DECISIONS.md holds what was ruled, dated, with the reason. New rulings are appended, and old ones are left as they were.
  • STATE.md holds where things are now, and is overwritten each time.

All four ride with the daimon on every round inside one budget, so what a diamond is told about itself is bounded and predictable. A crystal too large to fit whole keeps its overflow readable and searchable instead of dropping it. The counts on the card, Objectives, Open tasks と Done, are read off REQUIREMENTS.md.

Two kinds of fold

The word covers two different acts.

  • Summarise earlier messages, in the chat header, is the app compressing a conversation that no longer fits. Its decisions are filed into DECISIONS.md, its open items into REQUIREMENTS.md, and its next step into STATE.md. What it files can be found afterwards, and what it leaves in a notice cannot.
  • Fold now, on the Crystal face, is a model rewriting what this diamond remembers from what has happened since. Every write snapshots a version.

Filing is repeatable, so running the same fold twice files nothing twice, and the order two folds arrive in does not change what the files end up saying.

History

History lists every version, newest first. 表示 reads one, 復元 makes it current again, and 差分 shows the material a fold was given. Restore keeps what was current first, so a restore can itself be undone.

Acting without being asked

A diamond answers when you prompt it and at no other time, unless you set up a triggered action. Those live under When this diamond acts on the Crystal face, and Add an action makes one. Two things can set one off, either minutes of your own activity, counted only while you work, or mail arriving in a folder you name.

Each action carries an instruction, sent every time, and a context, sent once until you change it. They are an ordinary file, triggers.json, in the diamond's folder.

A new action is stopped, and says so: Stopped. Press ▶ on the light beside the pulldown to let this action run. One press arms it.

The light

Every action, every diamond and the すべて row wear the same three-part control, a play, a pause and a round light. The light says whether anything is armed and free to fire.

  • Red. Nothing here will act by itself.
  • Amber. Some of it will, and some is held.
  • Green. Everything set up will act.

A fresh account is red everywhere, which is the honest answer to whether anything is running by itself. An action missing its mailbox or its instruction is left out of the count, because it could not fire. Amber can only be arrived at, never set.

A held action spends nothing, because it is refused before the turn starts.

Tags, links and the Trash

タグ on the Crystal face files a diamond in the rail. Up to eight tags, lowercased and deduplicated. Suggested offers four to start from. Tags are inert. They are not sent to a model, they are no part of the memory, and they change nothing an agent does. タグで絞り込む in the rail narrows by them.

A diamond can also be linked to another, and the Graph is the picture of that.

Deleting a chat or a diamond trashes it. It is still stored and still synced, out of every daimon's reach, and kept thirty days with the date on the row. 復元 brings it back on every device. 完全に削除 と ゴミ箱を空にする are the two that ask.
