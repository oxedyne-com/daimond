チャット & Diamonds

A chat is a conversation with the daimon. A Diamond is a durable container for a pursuit, holding what you have worked out about a piece of work so that later work starts from it. Here: starting and reading chats, the turn controls, folding a chat into a Diamond, the workspace a Diamond works in, attaching files to either, and organising what you accumulate.

チャットを始める、チャットに戻る

新しいチャット を選びます。押すのは ＋ — 隣は チャット in the rail. Type in the box at the bottom of the centre panel and send with ➤。始めたチャットはすべて、レールの チャット, newest first; click one to bring it back to the centre. A new chat starts on your default model, and the selector in its header changes the model for that chat alone.

A chat tile carries a ×, and the ⋯ on the チャット head carries Delete all chats. Neither asks: both move chats to the Trash, where they can be brought back. The questions have moved to the two acts that cannot be undone, which are both in that panel.

回答が届いているあいだも入力を続けられます。そのとき送ったメッセージは失われるのではなく 待機中 rather than lost: it appears under the thread as a dashed bubble and goes as its own turn the moment the answer finishes. Click a queued bubble to take it back into the box and change it, or its × を押します。回答を止めるために ■ to stop the answer hands anything queued behind it back to you, unsent. Stopping means stopping.

長い会話を読む

A conversation grows quickly, so the chat header carries controls to keep it readable, at the top-right of the centre panel. Two more sit in the composer bar beside ➤.

ステップ

ステップ button shows or hides the tool steps in the thread — the work the agent did between your message and its answer. Hide them to read the conversation; show them to see what it did.

たたむ

− button collapses every answer, leaving what you asked. It is the quickest way to skim a long thread, and it is how you start picking turns to fold.

前のメッセージへ戻る

chevron up beside the send button jumps back to your last message. Press it again to walk back through the ones before it.

いちばん下へ

chevron down returns to the bottom of the chat and starts the walk again, so the next jump back goes to your last message instead of carrying on from where you had got to.

Concise

A standing toggle asking for short answers in this chat. It is a skill and not a hidden instruction: what it asks for is a file in your workspace you can read and edit.

Permission mode

A word beside the model saying what Daimond does without asking. Covered on 端末でのコマンド実行.

チャットを Diamond にたたみ込む

When a conversation has worked something out, you fold it: keep what you learnt, drop the back-and-forth that produced it. What you fold into is a Diamond, and two units make it up:

  • たたみ込み is one improvement, a single step forward.
  • 結晶 は、たたみ込みが積み上げてきたものです。仕事のかたち、順序どおりの手順、そして苦労して見つけた確認事項。Diamond とは、結晶と、それを生んだたたみ込みの連なりを合わせたものです。

やり取りを選んでたたむ

スレッドを −, then pick the turns worth keeping. A row of controls appears in the header: すべて選択, 選択を解除 と 選択分をたたむ, which folds the chosen turns into the Diamond. Fold a step you have 採り入れた and not a passing remark: folding keeps what you learnt, not the transcript.

ひとりでに取り込まれるものはありません。 結晶が裏で育つことはありません。差し出されたやり取りを選ぶのはあなたで、たたみ込み自体も提案です — エージェントが結晶を書き直し、その結果をあなたに見せ、あなたが受け入れるか拒否します。結晶が変わるのは、あなたがそう言ったときだけです。

Diamond を扱う

Diamonds はレールでチャットの上に並びます。追加するときに押すのは ＋ — 隣は Diamonds. Selecting one replaces the chat in the centre with its command surface, a compact view where you steer the work and fold further improvements into it. You can tell which face the panel is showing without reading it: on a Diamond it takes the Daimond mark beside the name and squares its corners, against the rounded corners of everything else on screen.

A Diamond has two faces, and a switch in the header moves between them. Crystal is what the Diamond knows. Chat is its own conversation, which persists like any other and carries a Fold button, because folding is done mid-flow rather than while configuring something. Pick a chat in the rail and the ordinary conversation returns, as it does whenever the stage would otherwise be empty.

Diamond がすること、しないこと

A Diamond retains; it does not run. Nothing executes a crystal: no workflow engine, no schedule, no chain from one Diamond to another, no saved job waiting to fire. Returning to a Diamond does not set it going, and neither does leaving it alone.

つまり また手に取る means steering it again with everything it has learnt already in hand. The crystal is prose, which is what a model reads best, and it is handed to the daimon when you work the Diamond and to every worker dispatched from it. What you worked out once reaches the work again without you retyping it, or remembering that you should.

結晶とその履歴

The crystal is two real files in the Diamond's own folder in your workspace: crystal.jsonは Diamond が知っていることであり crystal.html, the page that draws it. Open them, read them, edit them yourself, like any other file.

Every write snapshots a version, so a crystal has a history and nothing is written over. The history lists every version, newest first, each with what produced it:

  • 表示 — そのバージョンを読みます。
  • 復元 — そのバージョンをもう一度現在のものにします。
  • 差分 — たたみ込みが取り込んだ生の入力、つまりエージェントが縮めるために与えられた材料を見ます。
復元しても失われません。 現在だった文章は先に履歴へ残るので、古いバージョンを復元しても履歴は置き換わらず、増えます — 意図しない復元も、それ自体を取り消せます。

成果物

A Diamond also keeps track of what its work touched: the files written and the pages opened while you were working on it. None of it is yours to record. Daimond already notes every tool an agent uses, so the list is read off what happened, and gathered at the moment you accept a fold.

ただ 読んだ are left out on purpose. An agent may open forty files looking for one thing, and all forty listed would bury the one that mattered. What is kept is what the work produced, and the pages you asked to see.

Diamond に成果物があるときは、導く欄の上の行がその数を伝えます。クリックすると一覧が開き、各項目は三つのことをします。

  • 名前 はそれを開きます — ファイルは文書パネルに、ページはウェブパネルに。
  • 小さな矢印 は、それへの参照を導く欄に置きます。パスを打たずに「これの数字をやり直して」と言えます。
  • ×印 は、やはりこの Diamond のものではなかったときに取り除きます。
その後に名前を変えたり削除したりしたファイルは、開こうとしたときに そう告げます — 黙って何もしないのではありません。切れたリンクをそうでないふりで並べる一覧は、一覧がないより悪いからです。

Diamond の作業領域

Diamond には 作業領域: the files and folders you keep with it. You put them there. A daimon cannot add to its own workspace, so it works with what it has been given and asks you when it needs more.

これはあなたのファイルの見え方であって、ファイルを入れておく箱ではありません。 Nothing is copied when you attach something: the file stays where it is and the Diamond points at it. Attach the same folder to two Diamonds and there is still one folder, and an edit made through either is the file the other reads. Taking something out of a Diamond's workspace only detaches it; the file itself is untouched, and only a deliberate delete removes it. The word 作業領域 invites you to expect a copy, and there is none.

A Diamond also has a directory of its own, always in its workspace and always writable, so its daimon has somewhere to work before you have attached anything; the crystal lives there. A folder can be attached to be 編集ではなく参照のために添えることもできます。そうすると daimon は読めますが、書き込みはすべて拒まれます。そして 作業領域 パネルは、あなたのファイルすべてを見せるか、この Diamond が持つものだけを見せるか、目の前の仕事に合うほうを示します。

Attaching, with the paperclip

📎 on every row of the Workspace panel — on files as much as folders — and in the Doc panel's header reads Attach to current focus. One control, whose effect follows what is in focus and not which row it sits on. With nothing in focus it is not offered at all.

  • A Diamond is in focus. The file or folder joins that Diamond's workspace and stays until you take it off. Its daimon may then open it.
  • An ordinary chat is in focus. The attachment is for the next turn only, shown in a footer above the box that clears when the turn is sent.

Each attachment carries one of two states, toggled on its tile. Note says the path is worth knowing about, and costs a few tokens. Read says the contents are wanted now, and a file can run to thousands. Note is where an attachment starts, being much the cheaper: a default that spends your money unasked would be the wrong one.

Either way the text it generates — Note …, or Read … in full. — is written into the box in front of your message, to edit or delete before sending. A prompt the app writes and hides is a prompt nobody can argue with.

The footer carries a + for attaching several things without leaving what you are reading, and a toggle between the tile Stack and rows of larger Icons. Past six tiles — four in a short window — it scrolls instead of growing, so the composer stays put. An attachment made in a workspace that is not the open one says where it lives rather than reading as an empty folder, and is left out of the generated text: a path a model cannot open buys a turn spent apologising.

daimon が開けるもの

daimon が開けるのは、自分の Diamond の作業領域にあるファイルだけです。 Nothing outside it is readable or writable, by the daimon or by any worker it dispatches. This is no instruction in a prompt that a model may or may not honour: the check sits in the compiled code, at the one door every file tool passes through, and that code is in the published bundle, which is built reproducibly. So it is a claim you can check and not one to take on trust.

What it does not cover belongs in the same breath. It settles what a daimon can reach, not where what it reads ends up: whatever it opens goes to your model provider with the rest of the turn. It does guard against an agent that has been talked into something — a turn that has just read a stranger's instructions in a page or an email, and gone looking on their behalf, still reaches nothing outside this workspace. It is no guard against a provider misusing what you send it, or a build that is not the one we published; those are answered by ブラウザから出ていくもの and the すべてのビルドの封印された記録.

ワーカーを送り出す

Some tasks want work done and not merely recorded. For those the Diamond's daimon dispatches a ワーカーエージェント. Each worker runs in its own context with the workspace file tools, and is given the standing instructions and this Diamond's crystal. That crystal is the whole of what it knows about your pursuit: a worker cannot see your conversation, so the task it is handed must say everything else it needs.

同時に動くワーカーは最大で 八 workers run at once; the rest queue. The daimon dispatches them and its turn ends there, without receiving their output. Each reports a summary, and you fold the ones worth keeping in by hand, which is what keeps the choice of what enters a crystal yours. Live runs appear in the エージェント パネルに出ます。

エージェントを止める、停止する

エージェントパネルのヘッダーには、 すべての エージェントに一度に効く操作が三つあります。同じ三つは各タイルにも付いていて、ひとつだけに効かせることもできます。

  • 一時停止 ⏸ — hang up what is running and hold the queue. A running agent keeps what it had produced; a waiting one does not start. Nothing new launches until you resume. This is the brake for a fan-out spending faster than you meant.
  • 再開 ▶ — continue every paused agent from where it left off, picking up its own work instead of starting over, so a pause costs nothing but the wait.
  • 停止 ✕ — エージェントをそのまま終わらせます。それぞれできたところまでを、停止したタイルとして残します。 消去 removes them. The instant kill switch.

Each tile shows its 動いている間の費用, the tokens and dollars accruing live rather than only at the end, so you can see the spend while deciding whether to hold it. Under the header a line reads how many agents are 実行中, 一時停止中 と 待機中, which makes the size and the cost of a fan-out legible at a glance. The measures Daimond takes on its own are on the 支出 のページにあります。

A wider brake sits on the Everything row at the top of the rail. It pauses and resumes all of it at once: every Diamond, every chat, the workers, the mailboxes and any page being fetched for you. A paused thing refuses a turn before it starts, so nothing is spent.

Two limits hold. A worker cannot dispatch another worker, so delegation goes one level and stops. And one Diamond is steered at a time, so no second Diamond works away while your back is turned.

タグ

Diamonds accumulate, so they can be tagged. A tag is a short label you choose, edited from タグ on a Diamond's crystal. Four are suggested to start from — person, project, topic と org — but Daimond imposes no taxonomy, and you can type whatever suits your work.

打った文字は整えられます。タグは小文字にされ、前後の空白が落とされ、重複が取り除かれます。ひとつの Diamond に付けられるタグは最大 8 tags on a Diamond and 24 文字です。タグはほかのすべてと同じように、この端末の、その Diamond 自身のメタ情報に保存されます。

編集画面には二つの箱があります。上は この Diamond のタグ、下は すべてのタグ です。上の箱のチップにある × は、そのタグをこの Diamond から外して下のたまりに戻します。そこで一度クリックすれば元に戻ります。下の箱のチップにある × in the lower box is a different act: it deletes the tag itself, from every Diamond filed under it. That one asks first, and says how many Diamonds it will change. The four starter suggestions carry no ×, being always offered.

タグは何もしません。それがタグの要点です。 タグはあなたの整理のためだけのものです。モデルに送られることはなく、結晶に入ることもなく、どのエージェントの動きも変えません。Diamond に 急ぎ とタグを付けても、扱いは何も変わりません。

In the rail, Filter by tag opens the tags you can narrow by, with the count of them on the button. Click a tag to show only Diamonds carrying it; click it again to hide them instead. With more than one chosen, All と Any decide how they combine, and 消去 empties the filter. There is no separate search box: a Diamond is found by what it is filed under, and two ways of narrowing one short list is one more than it needs. The list is ordered by most recently touched, so what you last worked on sits at the top.

The Trash

Deleting a chat or a Diamond does not destroy it. It is trashed: still stored, still synced, out of the rail, out of the finders and out of every daimon's reach, listed in the Trash panel in the dock with 復元 beside it. It is kept thirty days and then destroyed, and each row says the date.

Because the act can be taken back, nothing asks before it. The two questions live where they are true instead: Delete permanently on one item, and Empty trash, which names the count. Both are irreversible and both go from every device. A dialog in front of a reversible act teaches people to click through dialogs, and then the irreversible one is clicked through too.

The reversal travels. Trashed on one device is trashed on all of them, and restored on one is restored on all of them, whichever order the two devices act in and whichever order their parcels arrive. A device that was away for six weeks works out the same retention date from the same stamp when it comes back. A backup carries the trash with it, so restoring an old one cannot un-delete what you deleted since, nor re-delete what you have restored.
